# October 1, 2026

Copilot is an experiment we're running to make your life easier: no SQL, just plain English. Ask a question and it digs through your warehouse, then offers to build the chart, dashboard, metric, KPI or report that goes with the answer.

## Copilot — Ask Your Data a Question

A new page in the sidebar. Type your question; Copilot reads your warehouse tables, works out the query, runs it, and comes back with the number and the table behind it.

- **Follow-ups keep their place** — ask "and how does that compare to last month?" and it knows what "that" refers to.
- **See the query** — expand any answer to read the exact query behind it, to check the logic or pass it to a colleague.
- **"Worth checking" notes** — a missing filter, a suspicious zero: when Copilot spots something off about its own answer, it says so above the result rather than letting you find out later.
- **Conversations are saved** — each chat is titled automatically and kept, so you can pick up a question you asked last week.

## It Builds Things Too

Everything it makes is the real thing, with a link to open it where it lives.

- **Charts, metrics, and KPIs** — "a chart of surveys per district" gets you a saved chart in your Charts list, not a throwaway image.
- **Dashboards** — a new one, or add the chart it just made to one you already have. It asks which you'd prefer.
- **Reports** — snapshot a dashboard into a report from the conversation.
- **Questions about Dalgo itself** — ask how a feature works and it reads the documentation to answer.

## You Approve Every Step

- **A plain-language receipt** — *"Create the chart 'Surveys by district' (bar) from prod.surveys?"* Approve it or cancel it.
- **Mask personal data first** — when a query would return columns that might hold personal information, the card lists them with tick boxes. Tick the sensitive ones and those values are scrambled before anything is sent on.
- **Read-only, always** — Copilot cannot change, delete or write warehouse data. By design, not by policy.

## Getting Copilot Switched On

Still an experiment, so we're enabling it organisation by organisation. If you'd like it turned on for yours, say hello on <a href="https://discord.gg/d99RnSThT" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Discord</a> or email <a href="mailto:support@dalgo.org">support@dalgo.org</a>.

- **An admin flips the switch** — under **Settings → Copilot**. Turning it on confirms you're happy for questions and query results from your warehouse to be processed by an AI provider.
- **Teach it your vocabulary** — the Context box below holds the facts Copilot should know every time: *"'SHG' means self-help group"*, *"Our fiscal year runs April–March"*. It makes a sizeable difference to the answers.

## dbt Now Installs With Dalgo

- **Installed alongside Dalgo itself** — no separate environment of its own.
- **Upgraded, with both adapters** — dbt moves from 1.8 to 1.10, and the BigQuery and Postgres adapters install with it.
